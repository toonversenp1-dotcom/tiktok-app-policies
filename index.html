Video Autopilot

Automated AI-powered short-form video creation and multi-platform posting

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

Features

  • AI-generated scripts from trending topics (Gemini 2.5 Flash)
  • Natural voiceovers via Edge TTS (free)
  • Stock footage from Pexels API
  • Local video rendering with FFmpeg (1080x1920 portrait)
  • Multi-platform captions via Groq LLM
  • Automated posting to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X, Facebook, Telegram
  • Execution logging to Google Sheets
  • Runs on n8n (self-hosted or n8n.cloud)
